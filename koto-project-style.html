      About

      Ruichen (Cheryl) Ma is a Communication Designer graduated from the Parsons School of Design in New York.

      Her work explores emotion, memory and human connection through branding, interaction design, creative coding and visual storytelling. She creates work across digital and physical mediums developing projects that transform personal experiences into concrete systems, objects and narratives.

      Her recent work explores themes of sensitivity, emotional reflection, family relationships and everyday rituals, while experimenting with the intersection of design, technology and human-centered experiences.

      In addition to design, she's into photography, independent publishing, travel and scuba diving.
